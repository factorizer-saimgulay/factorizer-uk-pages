About Us

Factorizer is the UK-based Human-Machine Systems, Software Engineering and programme management company of an engineering group that has been manufacturing in İzmir since 2013 and supplying Europe's largest onshore and offshore wind turbine OEMs.

We build the engineering tools behind the systems that run production, and carry out the delivery work that puts them on the shop floor.

Since 2013
The group has been manufacturing in İzmir as Gempol.
Europe's largest wind turbine OEMs
Onshore and offshore, supplied by the group from its İzmir production.
United Kingdom
Human-Machine Systems, Software Engineering and programme management, from Factorizer.
Supply and shipping
We supply and ship to the UK, Europe, Asia, North America and Africa.

Why us

What you get from us.

  1. 01

    Delivery capabilities

    OEM-scale capacity for a single order. The production environment behind us has supplied Europe's largest onshore and offshore wind turbine OEMs since 2013, and it builds one-offs to the same standards and on the same lines as series work.

  2. 02

    Commercial conditions

    One contract with a UK company, and the work made in the group's own plant. The price, the date and the responsibility sit with one party, with nobody between you and the shop floor.

  3. 03

    Constructive cooperation during the process

    Your engineering contact is in the UK and sits in the same group as the plant. Questions go straight to the people cutting and welding the steel, and answers come back as drawings and dates, in days.
