What it is like

What the work actually involves.

Work that runs across disciplines

A programme here runs from the first specification to a machine working on a site, and the same people carry it through. Mechanical, controls, software and the person who supervises the result work as one project, with shared ownership.

Small enough that the work is yours

Decisions are made close to the work. What you design is what gets built, and you are there when it is commissioned and when it comes back for improvement.

First-of-a-kind work

Most of what we take on calls for something new. That means prototypes, iteration, and the discipline of proving a design before it is manufactured.

What we look for

The things that matter here.

  • Multidisciplinary engineers above all, because the best work happens where disciplines meet
  • Engineers who carry a problem from concept all the way to a machine that runs
  • People who write things down: a recorded design decision is one everyone can check
  • Comfort with learning early, which costs far less than learning at commissioning
  • Care about the person who ends up operating or supervising what you build

Formal qualifications are read alongside what matters most to us: what you have built, what you learned from it, and what you did next. If something would be new to you, say so; much of this work is done by people doing something for the first time, carefully.

We hold ourselves to the same terms we require of everyone who supplies into our work: lawful and voluntary employment, a chain free of forced and child labour, and the freedom to raise a concern safely. They are set out under conditions of doing business with us.

Applying

How an application is handled.

Send what you have: a CV, a portfolio, a repository, drawings, a description of a machine you built. Tell us which part of it was yours. One piece of work described in depth tells us the most.

What you send is used only to consider you, and stays within Factorizer Ltd. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the same address. The privacy policy sets out how long we keep it and what your rights are.
