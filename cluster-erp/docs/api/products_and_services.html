Products and services API (products_and_services)

HTTP endpoints of the products_and_services module under /api/v1/products_and_services: 55 routes with permissions, schemas, status codes, and error codes.

The products_and_services module serves unit categories and units with conversion, product categories with a tree, products, product models, product versions, photos, and product vendors. All 55 endpoints sit under /api/v1/products_and_services. For business meaning see the Products and services module page.
