Intranet events

Reference for the events sub-module of intranet, with company events, invitations, RSVPs, capacity, change notifications, visibility, and media.

Contents

  • Event fields
  • Statuses and actions

  • Other modules
  • Terms introduced
  • Related pages

This page describes the events sub-module in src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/. An intranet event (IntranetEvent) is a company event with invitations, answers (RSVPs), an optional capacity with a waiting list, and a log of every change. The rules live as pure functions in events/lifecycle.py; the service loads rows, asks those functions, and writes back (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:1-21).

Scope and source¶

  • Defined in: src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py (IntranetEvent, IntranetEventInvitation, IntranetEventChange, IntranetEventRsvp, IntranetMedia), events/lifecycle.py, events/services.py, events/routes.py, events/schemas.py, and events/metadata.py.
  • Count: 5 tables, 15 routes in events/routes.py, 6 actions built by the helper _action in events/metadata.py (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/metadata.py:45-55, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/metadata.py:202-208).

An invitation (IntranetEventInvitation) records the source everyone, segment, direct, or team, and the revision the person was last told about. An answer (IntranetEventRsvp) is going, maybe, or declined, with a waiting-list flag. A change row (IntranetEventChange) records the revision, the action, the actor, the changed fields, and whether the change was material (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:1201-1298, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:62-63).

Statuses and actions¶

The stored statuses are draft, published, cancelled, and archived. completed is derived: a published event whose end has passed reads as completed, and nothing is written (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:33-48, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:164-174).

In prose: an event is drafted, published, and may be cancelled with a reason the invitees can read. A cancellation can be undone within 24 hours. Every state except archived can be archived, and Restore to draft brings it back (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:42-59, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:303-311).

Action Label Condition Effect Source
publish Publish status is draft builds invitations, may notify invitees, emits IntranetEventPublished src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:457-474, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:587-600

A seventh action, invite, adds direct invitations and is not declared in metadata (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:51-53, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:553-570).

Rules¶

  • A draft is edited freely; a published event is edited with a revision bump; a cancelled or archived event is not editable (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:55-57, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:356-442).
  • Each edit writes a change row with the field diff. A change of time or place is material: every invitee and attendee is told and anyone who answered is asked whether the answer still holds. Other changes reach attendees only. Organisers are never told about their own edits (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:65-68, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:207-228).
  • Only going takes a seat. A full event with a waiting list waitlists the answer; a full event without one refuses it with INTRANET_EVENT_FULL (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:231-264).
  • An answer is accepted only on a published event with answers enabled (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:605-615).
  • Visibility: organisers and holders of the oversight bypass see everything. Others never see drafts, archived events, or private events; an invite-only event needs an invitation; an open event needs the audience or an invitation (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:266-288).
  • Attendee names are shown to everyone, to attendees only, or to organisers only, by attendee_list_visibility (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/lifecycle.py:290-301).
  • GET /events/{id}/ics returns a calendar file built from the event (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/routes.py:295-322, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:739-770).

Other modules¶

GetUpcomingIntranetEvents answers what a person may see of the company calendar in the coming days, with the same visibility rules (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/module.py:291-303, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:709-737). The events IntranetEventPublished, IntranetEventUpdated, and IntranetEventCancelled are emitted (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:432, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:498, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/events/services.py:591). A subscribe call for them was not found in src/.
