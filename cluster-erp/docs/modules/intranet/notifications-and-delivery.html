Intranet notifications and delivery

How the intranet inbox stores notifications, fans them out to channels including e-mail, and how official document delivery records a receipt.

This page explains three parts of the intranet module that carry messages to employees. The notifications sub-module in src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/ is the in-app inbox. src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notify.py is the helper that raises a notification and hands the e-mail channel to the mail module. The delivery sub-module in src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/ hands official documents to employees and records the receipt.

In short¶

  • A notification is stored once and fanned out as one dispatch row per channel: in-app is delivered at once, e-mail goes through the system mailbox, push and SMS are recorded as skipped (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:1-9).
  • Read state lives on the server as seen_at and read_at (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:193-231).
  • Notifications carry an English fallback title plus an i18n key with parameters, so the client renders them in the reader's language (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:53-58).
  • An official delivery moves from pending to delivered to acknowledged or refused, and the acknowledgement records who, when, and from which client address (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:26-30, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:221-243).

How it works¶

Notification path¶

In prose:

  1. An intranet service calls notify_party on its own session, so the notification commits with the action that caused it. The helper opens the row-level backstop for exactly the recipient for this insert (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notify.py:1-6, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notify.py:68-113).
  2. Another module can publish IntranetNotify on the EventBus. The handler writes into the caller's tenant database and commits (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/module.py:305-345, src/cluster_erp/core/contracts.py:2194-2208).
  3. NotificationService.create inserts the notification and one dispatch per requested channel (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:37-99).
  4. For the e-mail channel, fan_out_email asks the parties module for the person's addresses through ResolveEntityEmails, takes the primary one, and asks the mail module to send through SendTransactionalEmail (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notify.py:20-65).
  5. The e-mail dispatch is marked sent when the system mailbox accepted the message, otherwise skipped with the reason, for example no address or no handler. sent means an attempt, not a delivery receipt (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:111-135).

The dispatch states are queued, sent, delivered, bounced, failed, and skipped; the channels are in_app, email, push, and sms (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:82-90). How the mail module sends is on mail module.

Official delivery¶

A delivery (IntranetOfficialDelivery) has a code from the sequence intranet_official_delivery, a recipient party, a title, a category, a confidentiality flag, a link to a document version or an attachment, a period, a channel, and a signature requirement (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:936-1015, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:130-154). The categories are payslip, contract, certificate, disciplinary, commendation, notice, id_card, and other (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:125-134).

In prose: a delivery starts pending. Deliver sets delivered_at and notifies the employee in-app and by e-mail with the key intranet.notif_delivery. Acknowledge Receipt records the time, the client address, and an optional signature attachment; only the addressee can acknowledge. Refuse records a refusal (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:176-244).

Action Label Condition Source
download_document Open Document none src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/metadata.py:98-106
deliver Deliver status is pending src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/metadata.py:107-115
acknowledge Acknowledge Receipt status is delivered src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/metadata.py:116-124
refuse Refuse status is delivered src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/metadata.py:125-133

GET /deliveries/{id}/document streams the file only after the row-level check of the delivery. It resolves a linked document version to its file through ResolveDocumentVersionFile on the EventBus (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/routes.py:120-164).

Why it is built this way¶

  • The notify.py docstring names in-app as the guaranteed channel and e-mail and push as best-effort channels that never claim delivery (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notify.py:1-6).
  • The dispatch model docstring states that the interface must not treat an unacknowledged notification as undelivered (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:655-660).
  • The delivery docstring states that publishing centrally is not enough; the employee must be shown to have received the document (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:1-4).

Rules and boundaries¶

  • The categories payslip, disciplinary, and contract are confidential unless the creator says otherwise (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:37, src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:130-135).
  • A confidential delivery is visible only to its recipient and to holders of intranet_official_delivery.read_all, not to the recipient's manager (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:40-64).
  • Creating a delivery needs intranet_official_delivery.create; deliver and refuse need .update; acknowledge needs .read (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/routes.py:84-239).
  • Deleting a delivery sets deleted_at (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/delivery/services.py:246-251).

The contract list is on the contracts reference. Platform-wide notification rules are on notification rules.

Limits and open points¶

  • A construction of IntranetNotify outside src/cluster_erp/core/contracts.py was not found in src/; inside the module, notifications are raised through notify_party.
  • Push and SMS dispatches are recorded as skipped with the error channel not configured (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/notifications/services.py:90-96).
  • The dispatch row has next_retry_at and attempt_no, but a retry job was not found in src/ or deploy/ (src/cluster_erp/modules/intranet/models.py:655-686).
