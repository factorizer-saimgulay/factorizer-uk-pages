Inventory module (inventory)

Module page for inventory, the warehouses, storage locations, stock levels per product breakdown, goods movements, reservations, batches, and serial numbers.

The inventory module in src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/ keeps the stock of the company. It holds warehouses and storage locations, one stock level row per product, model, version, place, batch, owning project, and custodian, and the goods movements that change those rows. It also manages stock reservations, usage decisions on received goods, project stock loans, batches, and serial numbers. Purchasing, production, nesting, and sales post their stock changes to this module through the EventBus.

What the module does¶

  • Keeps warehouses with a code that is unique among live rows, and their storage locations (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/warehouses/models.py:13-71).
  • Keeps stock per product, model, and version. The quantity is split into six categories: unrestricted, reserved, quality, blocked, blocked at goods receipt, and returns (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/models.py:25-123).
  • Posts goods movements of 13 types and writes one movement row per change (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:78-92).
  • Reserves unrestricted stock for a reference and later consumes or cancels the reservation (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1901-2080).
  • Records a usage decision on a received quantity and posts the follow-up movement (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1496-1666).
  • Moves stock between a project stripe and common stock, and lends stock between stripes with a payback (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:542-800).
  • Tracks batches with origin, expiry, and status, and serial numbers with status (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/batches/models.py:14-125).
  • Serves a warehouse dashboard read model (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/warehouses/services.py:145).

Business rules¶

  • Every posting first asks GetProductById and refuses a product that is not stockable (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:2119-2134).
  • A batch-managed product needs a batch, and a serial-tracked product needs a serial number. A blocked or expired batch is refused (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:2136-2193).
  • A goods receipt adds to the unrestricted quantity of the matching stock row and creates the row when it is missing. The row is read with a row lock (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:2195-2286).
  • A goods receipt that names a purchase order as reference is refused; purchase order receipts go through the procurement receive flow (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/routes.py:166-178).
  • A decrease takes the requested quantity from the given project stripe. A goods issue may take the rest from common stock and then writes one movement per stripe; the other decreases refuse a shortfall (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:2288-2363, src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:473-490).
  • A transfer between two project stripes is refused unless the setting allow_project_to_project_stock_transfer is on (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:552-566).
  • A project loan needs two different sides, at least one of them a project (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:615-640).
  • An adjustment sets the unrestricted quantity to the new value and records the difference as the movement quantity (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1051-1100).
  • Stock categories move only along nine allowed pairs, for example gr_blocked to unrestricted or quality to blocked (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:112-122).
  • A usage decision applies only to a goods receipt or a blocked goods receipt, and not beyond the undecided quantity. accept and conditional_accept move to unrestricted, reject returns to the vendor, rework moves to blocked, and scrap posts a scrap movement (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:124-135, src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1496-1625).
  • A reservation moves quantity from unrestricted to reserved. Consuming removes it from reserved; cancelling returns it to unrestricted (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1901-2080).
  • A movement can be reversed once, and a reversal movement cannot be reversed (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1730-1760).
  • Batch origin is customer, supplier, or internal (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/batches/services.py:35).
  • Movement numbers come from the yearly sequence goods_movement, decisions from usage_decision, and loans from stock_loan (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:383).

Privacy, retention, and history¶

The module has no privacy_handlers.py. Warehouse, location, batch, and serial deletes set deleted_at (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/warehouses/services.py:137-143, src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/batches/services.py:178-184). Movements have no delete route; a wrong movement is reversed by a counter movement (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/routes.py:376-450, src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1730). Changes are captured by change history.

Analytics facts¶

analytics_facts.py contributes one fact set, goods_movement, over inventory_goods_movements. Its dimensions are movement type, product, warehouse, party, reference type, reversal flag, and posting date. Its measures are movement_count and total_qty, read with goods_movement.read (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/analytics_facts.py:29-104). Stock levels are not contributed; the docstring gives the reason (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/analytics_facts.py:1-8). See analytics facts.

Settings and reference data¶

The module has no settings section. The project-to-project transfer switch is the server setting allow_project_to_project_stock_transfer, default False, read through Settings (src/cluster_erp/config/settings.py:184, src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:561); see settings reference.

Discrepancies¶

Note

qty is a positive magnitude for receipts, issues and transfers; direction comes from movement_type. An adjustment is the exception: it writes the difference between the counted and the booked quantity as qty, which is negative when stock goes down (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/stock/services.py:1075-1087). A total over mixed movement types therefore measures activity volume, not net stock change (src/cluster_erp/modules/inventory/analytics_facts.py:10-15).
