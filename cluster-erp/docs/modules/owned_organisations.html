Owned organisations module (owned_organisations)

Module page for owned_organisations, which holds the company's own organisations, their departments, the employees, and their assignments.

The owned_organisations module in src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/ holds the organisations that the company itself owns, such as a head office and branches, with their department trees. It also holds the company-level employee record of each person and the assignments that place a person in an organisation, a department, and a position. Every organisation and every employee is a role of a party; see party unification.

What the module does¶

  • Records each owned organisation with a name, a description, a founding date, a head-office flag, and a default IANA time zone (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/models.py:21-51).
  • Records departments as a tree inside an organisation (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/departments/models.py:16-39).
  • Keeps one company-level employee record per person, with employment, statutory, diet, emergency-contact, and manager fields (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/models.py:18-66).
  • Places an employee in one or more organisations through assignments with a position, an employment type, and dates (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/models.py:69-108).
  • Answers other modules' questions about the head office, the company time zone, the directory, and a manager's team (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:90-99).

Screens and metadata¶

The module declares no sidebar group of its own; its organisations are a tab of the parties list (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:440-442, src/cluster_erp/modules/parties/module.py:589-597). Organisation, employee, and assignment declare IdentityMeta, so opening one opens the party card (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:53, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:355, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:590). The organisation detail has the tabs General, Departments (a tree), and Employees (the organisation's assignments) (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:90-110). Departments use a tree view under their organisation (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:252-281). Six employee fields carry permission="employee.read_sensitive": tax number, national id, social security number, blood group, diet preferences, and diet note (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:469-549).

Business rules¶

  • An organisation created without a party_id gets one through EnsurePartyForOrganisation; if no party comes back, the create fails (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/services.py:74-94).
  • default_timezone must be a known IANA zone name or empty (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/schemas.py:13-19).
  • Creating an employee for a party that already has a live employee record returns the existing record (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/services.py:63-66).
  • An employee needs an emergency contact name and phone on create (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/schemas.py:47-49).
  • An assignment may name the party instead of the employee; the service then finds or creates the person's employee record (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/services.py:194-203, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/services.py:78-86).
  • Employment types are full_time, part_time, contract, intern, and freelance; blood groups are the eight ABO and Rh groups (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/schemas.py:11-29).
  • Fields gated by employee.read_sensitive are removed from responses and ignored on input for a caller without the code (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/routes.py:57-78); see field and row access.
  • Codes come from the yearly sequences owned_organisation, department, employee, and assignment (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/services.py:71, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/departments/services.py:46, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/services.py:89, src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/employees/services.py:214).

Three answers carry rules worth knowing:

  • The head office is the organisation flagged is_headquarters, else the oldest live one (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:251-277).
  • The company time zone is the given organisation's own zone, then the head office's, then any active organisation's, ordered by head-office flag and id. A warning is logged when several organisations are flagged as head office (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:279-343). The working time page explains where the zone is used.
  • A manager's team is every live, active employee below the manager in the manager_party_id chain, found with a recursive query that stops on cycles (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:197-226).

Privacy, retention, and history¶

privacy_handlers.py reports the employment records a person holds. It anonymises identifiers, health data, and emergency-contact fields only when the request carries include_employment_records, because employment records have statutory retention duties, as its docstring states (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/privacy_handlers.py:1-9). When a party is deactivated, its employee records are soft-deleted with the reason party_deactivated; reactivating the party restores exactly those rows (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:348-420). See privacy and data-subject requests and change history.

Analytics facts¶

This module has no analytics_facts.py.

Settings and reference data¶

The module has no settings section and seeds nothing at start-up (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/module.py:31-33). Organisations, departments, employees, and assignments accept tenant-defined fields in extra (src/cluster_erp/modules/owned_organisations/organisations/metadata.py:36-48); see tenant fields.
