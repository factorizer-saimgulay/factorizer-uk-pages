Procurement module (procurement)

Module page for procurement, with suppliers, purchase requests, purchasing RFQs, purchase orders, contracts, info records, critical materials, substitution, and MRP.

The procurement module in src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/ covers buying. It holds the supplier facet of a party, purchase requests, purchasing RFQs with vendor quotations and scoring, purchase orders with goods receipt, purchase contracts, and purchasing info records. It also runs material requirements planning (MRP) and keeps critical materials and substitution groups. This page is the overview; each sub-module has its own reference page, and MRP has an explanation page.

What the module does¶

  • Keeps a supplier facet per party, with identity fields derived from the party (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/suppliers/services.py:80-145).
  • Collects material needs in purchase requests that move through approval to an order (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_requests/services.py:76-83).
  • Sends purchasing RFQs to several vendors, records and scores their quotations, and awards lines, which creates one purchase order per awarded vendor (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_rfqs/services.py:1215-1292).
  • Places purchase orders, receives goods per line, and posts the receipt to inventory (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:695-837).
  • Keeps quantity and value contracts and a price master per vendor and product breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/models.py:23-109, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/models.py:25-121).
  • Runs MRP in seven phases and turns its proposals into requests or orders (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/mrp/engine.py:1-19).
  • Flags critical materials and groups materials that can replace each other (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/critical_materials/models.py:12-65, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/substitution_groups/models.py:24-127).

Data model¶

Each sub-module page lists its fields. All material lines carry product_id, product_model_id, and product_version_id; order lines may also be text items without a product (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/schemas.py:20-37). Money columns are Numeric, for example the order total Numeric(19, 4) and the unit price Numeric(18, 4) (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/models.py:81-82, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/models.py:195-200). Line totals are rounded with ROUND_HALF_EVEN before storage (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:307-312).

Screens and metadata¶

get_metadata declares a sidebar group Procurement (Turkish Satın Alma) with 19 sections (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:1072-1418). Seven sections are custom widgets of the gated bundle: MRP Dashboard, Exception Workbench, Planned Orders, Stock Requirements, Segmentation (XYZ), DDMRP Buffers, and ATP/CTP. The others are lists: Forecast (PIR), MRP Runs, Safety Time Periods, Quota Arrangements, Purchase Requests, Purchasing RFQs, Purchase Orders, Purchase Contracts, Info Records, Critical Materials, and Substitution Groups. The RFQ comparison is the eighth widget, purchase_rfq_comparison (gated_apps/procurement/app.js:1647-1656). See gated app bundles and desktop module screens.

Status and actions¶

Statuses and actions are listed on each sub-module page. The diagram shows the main path from a requirement to stock.

In prose: an MRP run creates purchase requests from its items (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/mrp/services.py:331). A request can go to an RFQ through purchase_request_id (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_rfqs/models.py:49), or straight to an order with POST /purchase-orders/from-pr/{pr_id} once approved (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:349-413). Awarding RFQ lines creates one order per awarded vendor (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_rfqs/services.py:1281-1286). Receiving an order line posts a goods receipt to inventory with PostGoodsReceipt (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:799-836). An award of a supplier quotation in the purchasing_quotations module updates the info records (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:813-862).

Business rules¶

The rules of each sub-module are on its page. Two rules apply across the module:

  • Every material line is checked with ensure_product_breakdown through the EventBus (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:279, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_requests/services.py:233).
  • Documents use yearly sequences: supplier, purchase_request, purchase_order, purchase_contract, info_record, substitution_group, and mrp_pir (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/suppliers/services.py:130, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_requests/services.py:208, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:245).

Permissions¶

Each sub-module page lists its keys. The module checks the families supplier.*, purchase_request.*, purchase_rfq.*, purchase_order.*, purchase_contract.*, info_record.*, critical_material.*, substitution_group.*, and mrp_run.* with require_permission in the route files.

Privacy, retention, and history¶

privacy_handlers.py answers data-subject requests for supplier facets of individual parties: CollectSubjectData returns the rows, and AnonymiseSubject overwrites the identifying values and deactivates the rows (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/privacy_handlers.py:1-11). Deletes set deleted_at (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/suppliers/services.py:185-191). See privacy and data-subject requests and change history.

Analytics facts¶

analytics_facts.py contributes two fact sets. purchase_order over procurement_purchase_orders has the measures order_count, total_amount, average_amount, and median_amount, with currency as the currency field. purchase_request over procurement_purchase_requests has request_count and total_estimated_cost (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/analytics_facts.py:19-179). See analytics facts.

Settings and reference data¶

The module has no settings section. MRP reads lot-sizing defaults per ABC class from Settings (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/mrp/engine.py:3037-3048); see settings reference.

Discrepancies¶

Warning

Discrepancy. The project's written engineering rules forbid fixed values and require currency to come from reference data. The purchase order currency defaults to "TRY" in the model, the schema, and two services, and the purchase request schema has the same default (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/models.py:78-79, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/schemas.py:111, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:403, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_rfqs/services.py:1326, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_requests/schemas.py:63, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:801). This page documents the code.
