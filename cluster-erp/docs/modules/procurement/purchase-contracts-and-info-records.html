Purchase contracts and info records

Reference for purchase_contracts and info_records in procurement, with fields, statuses, actions, routes, price history, and how awards update prices.

This page describes two sub-modules of src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/: purchase_contracts/, the outline agreements with a vendor for a period, and info_records/, the price master per vendor and product breakdown with its price history. Supplier quotations that feed both are described on the purchasing quotations module.

Scope and source¶

  • Defined in: models.py, services.py, routes.py, and metadata.py of purchase_contracts and info_records.
  • Count: 4 tables; 16 contract routes and 8 info record routes (@router. decorators); 5 ActionMeta entries, all on contracts.

Contract statuses and actions¶

The service maps each action to a target status and the allowed source statuses (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:39-45).

In prose: activate needs a complete draft: at least one item, a validity period with from not after to, and a target value for a value contract (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:234-256, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:308-346). Every transition checks the record version for concurrent edits (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:213-232).

Constraints and validation¶

  • A contract changes only in draft; only draft or cancelled contracts can be deleted (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:36, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_contracts/services.py:198-210).
  • One live info record exists per vendor and product breakdown; a manual duplicate is refused (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/models.py:33-41, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/services.py:160-173).
  • upsert creates a record or updates the price, reactivates the record, and appends a history row when the price changed (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/services.py:229-292).
  • On SupplierQuotationAwarded, the module upserts one info record per awarded line that carries a full breakdown, each line in its own savepoint (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:813-862).
  • CreateFollowOnFromSupplierQuotation creates a purchase order or a contract from an awarded supplier quotation; text lines become text items (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:711-810).
  • Purchase order items take their default unit price from lookup_price with the full breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:297-303).

Discrepancies¶

Warning

Discrepancy. The project's written engineering rules forbid a silent fallback from model and version to the product. The route GET /info-record-lookup passes only vendor and product to lookup_price, which then returns the newest live record of any model and version of that product (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/routes.py:140-149, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/info_records/services.py:85-127). This page documents the code.
