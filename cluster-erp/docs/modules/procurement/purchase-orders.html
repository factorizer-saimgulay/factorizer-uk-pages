Purchase orders

Reference for purchase_orders in procurement, with order and item fields, statuses, actions, goods receipt into inventory, reversal, and delivery address.

This page describes the purchase_orders sub-module in src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/ and the shared helper src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/delivery_address.py. A purchase order (PurchaseOrder) is an order placed with a vendor. Receiving an order line posts a goods receipt in the inventory module through the EventBus.

Scope and source¶

  • Defined in: models.py (PurchaseOrder, PurchaseOrderItem), services.py (PurchaseOrderService), routes.py, metadata.py, and item_metadata.py of the sub-module.
  • Count: 2 tables, 18 routes (@router. decorators in routes.py), 6 ActionMeta entries (5 on the order, 1 on the item).

Statuses and actions¶

The service allows these transitions (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:67-75):

In prose: an order starts as draft. send needs an order date and the flag has_technical_attachment (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:620-641). Receiving is allowed in acknowledged and in_transit; when every live line is received, the order becomes received (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:705-774). A receipt reversal can move a received order back to in_transit (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:854-891). close follows received; cancel is open until receipt (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:892-929).

Goods receipt¶

The diagram shows a receipt of one line.

In prose: the service refuses a text item and an item without model and version, because stock is posted per breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:718-731). The received quantity may not exceed the ordered quantity (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:738-749). The service updates the line and order status, emits PoReceivedFull or PoReceivedPartial, and then queries PostGoodsReceipt with the order as reference, the line id, and the order's project as stock owner (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:750-822). An error from inventory fails the receipt with PO_GOODS_RECEIPT_FAILED; a missing inventory handler is logged and the receipt stays a purchasing record only (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:823-837). A reversed goods movement gives the open quantity back through the query ReconcilePoReceiptReversal (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:192-202, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:885-916).

Constraints and validation¶

  • On create, the vendor is read with GetPartyById; a blacklisted vendor is refused (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:215-243).
  • Each catalogue item's breakdown is checked with ensure_product_breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:279, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:507).
  • Unit price is rounded to four places and the line total to the column scale, both with ROUND_HALF_EVEN; the header total is the sum (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:112-123, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:307-312).
  • The header and items change only in draft; only a draft can be deleted (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:415-468).
  • The delivery address must be a shipping address of the delivery party; its text is stored as a snapshot (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/delivery_address.py:24-28, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/delivery_address.py:78, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:251).
  • Events: PoCreated, PoSent, PoAcknowledged, PoReceivedFull, PoReceivedPartial, PoCancelled (src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:338, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:648, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:672, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:779-797, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/purchase_orders/services.py:921).
