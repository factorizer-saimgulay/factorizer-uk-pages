Routings and operation types

Reference for routings, operation types, routing templates, cut technology rules, and component allocations in production, with the eBOM scaffold that derives routings from the BOM.

Contents

This page describes the sub-modules of src/cluster_erp/modules/production/ that define how a product is made: routings/ (with the scaffold in routings/scaffold.py), operation_types/, routing_templates/ (with cut technology rules), and component_allocations/. A routing (Routing) is the operation sequence of one product breakdown. Its component allocations tie BOM items and other inputs to operations; together they form the operation BOM (eBOM). The BOM side is on the BOM module.

Scope and source¶

  • Defined in: the models.py, schemas.py, services.py, and routes.py of the four sub-modules, routings/scaffold.py (RoutingScaffolder), and routing_templates/metadata.py.
  • Count: 9 tables; 28 routing routes, 8 operation type routes, 16 routing template and rule routes, and 6 allocation routes (@router. and @rules_router. decorators).

Routing statuses¶

A routing moves from draft to active to obsolete (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/services.py:44-48). Activation is refused while blockers remain: an operation without a work center, without an operation type, or with zero run time per unit, or a prep operation without an operation type (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/services.py:541-599, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/services.py:610-649). GET /routings/{routing_id}/readiness returns these blockers (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/routes.py:319). When a new BOM version becomes active, live routings of the same breakdown are marked stale (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/module.py:195-224).

eBOM scaffold¶

The scaffold derives a routing from the BOM of one product breakdown. The diagram shows the plan and apply path.

In prose: scaffold-plan is a dry run that needs the full product breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/routes.py:155-206). The scaffolder reads the BOM tree with GetBomScaffoldTree (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:177-217). Non-phantom items become material inputs of one final joining operation; phantom items are never inputs, and their children are lifted with the phantom's quantity multiplied (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:8-12, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:247-335). A make-from-stock item with length stock and a cut length gets a mandatory cut prep step; sheet stock gets a 2D cut candidate (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:13-15, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:336-348). A routing template that matches the product's category and material type supplies the operation sequence; two equally specific templates are refused (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:22-28, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:470-540). A cut technology rule answers the cut question (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:29-31, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:589-622). Work centers come only from declared capabilities (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:16-21, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:388-469). Process operations are never created automatically unless a template declares them (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:22-24). The plan carries a plan_hash over the breakdown, BOM version, and item quantities (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:41-43, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:218-246).

scaffold-apply writes the plan in one transaction. A changed BOM gives a 409, a missing required answer a 422, and an existing live routing on the breakdown a 409 (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/routes.py:207-262, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:1038-1354). When an operation has an output, the scaffolder asks products_and_services to create or reuse an intermediate product with CreateScaffoldIntermediate (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:2234). Tree mode plans and applies sub-assemblies all or nothing (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:962-1037, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:1355-1425). copy-to deep-copies a routing onto another breakdown and re-binds allocations to the target's active BOM; unmatched rows are reported (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:35-39, src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/scaffold.py:2077-2460).

Constraints and validation¶

  • A component allocation is unique per BOM item and operation, per operation and source operation, and per operation and product, among live rows (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/component_allocations/models.py:27-56).
  • The bom module reads allocations through GetComponentAllocations to fill operation type and work center in its explosion (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/module.py:145-147).

Discrepancies¶

Warning

Discrepancy. The project's written engineering rules forbid a silent fallback from model and version to the product. The legacy routes scaffold-from-bom and scaffold-tree-from-bom take the product's default model and version from GetProductDefaultBreakdown when the body has no pair; the code comment limits this to the legacy endpoints (src/cluster_erp/modules/production/routings/services.py:146-177). This page documents the code.
