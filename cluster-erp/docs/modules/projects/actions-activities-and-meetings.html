Reference

Project actions, activities, and meetings

Reference for actions, activities, meetings, and kickoffs in projects, with fields, statuses, activity relations and material links, and meeting rules.

Contents

  • Scope and source
  • Fields
  • Statuses and actions
  • Routes
  • Constraints and validation
  • Terms introduced
  • Related pages

This page describes four sub-modules of src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/. actions/ holds the actions of a project, from meetings or ad hoc. activities/ holds the activities of a project schedule, their dependency relations, and their links to materials. kickoffs/ holds the single kick-off meeting of a project, and meetings/ the weekly follow-up meetings. The schedule itself is on schedules.

Scope and source¶

  • Defined in: models.py, schemas.py, services.py, routes.py, and metadata.py of actions, activities, kickoffs, and meetings.
  • Count: 6 tables; 8 action routes, 12 activity routes, 7 kick-off routes, and 6 meeting routes (@router. decorators); 3 ActionMeta entries.

Activity types are engineering, procurement, production, assembly, milestone, and cost; statuses are planned, in_progress, done, and cancelled; the earned-value technique is 0_100, 50_50, or percent_complete (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/schemas.py:10-33).

Statuses and actions¶

An action moves from open to in_progress, and from either to done or cancelled; done and cancelled are terminal (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/actions/services.py:20-25).

In prose: an action starts open, is worked on, and ends done or cancelled. The service refuses any other step (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/actions/services.py:160-170).

Constraints and validation¶

  • An activity needs an existing schedule, and its planned end may not precede its planned start (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/services.py:97-121).
  • A relation may not link an activity to itself, both activities must be on the same schedule, and a relation that would create a cycle is refused (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/services.py:154-211).
  • A material link needs a project item or a BOM item reference (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/services.py:221-243).
  • Activity changes emit ProjectActivityChanged (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/services.py:43-58).
  • A project has one live kick-off, and one live meeting per week number (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/kickoffs/models.py:20-27, src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/meetings/models.py:19-27).
  • mark_held stamps held_at once and emits ProjectKickoffCompleted (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/kickoffs/services.py:101-121).
  • GET /actions/overdue lists actions whose due time has passed (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/actions/services.py:51-70).
  • Creating an action emits ProjectActionCreated (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/actions/services.py:94).
  • Hours are Numeric: work quantity Numeric(10, 2), budgeted hours Numeric(12, 2), link quantity Numeric(14, 4) (src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/models.py:43-45, src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/models.py:71-73, src/cluster_erp/modules/projects/activities/models.py:132).
