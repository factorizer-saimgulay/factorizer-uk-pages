Purchasing quotations module (purchasing_quotations)

Module page for purchasing_quotations, the supplier quotation register with lines, pricing conditions, scales, versions, award, comparison, and follow-on documents.

The purchasing_quotations module in src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/ is the register of supplier quotations. A supplier quotation (PurchasingQuotation) records an offer from a vendor, with or without a purchasing RFQ, priced per line with discount, surcharge, and freight conditions. Awarding a quotation can create a purchase order or a purchase contract in procurement and updates the purchasing info records. The register also lists the vendor quotations kept inside the purchasing RFQ sub-module.

What the module does¶

  • Records supplier quotations with four source values: rfq, unsolicited (default), spot, and price_list (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/models.py:40-46, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/schemas.py:16).
  • Prices each line through a sequence of conditions into an effective unit price (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:797-831).
  • Keeps revisions: revise clones a quotation into a new version and marks the old one superseded (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:509-594).
  • Awards lines and, on request, creates a purchase order or contract through the EventBus (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:285-346).
  • Compares all live quotation lines for one product by effective price, and lists a register of native and RFQ quotations (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:990-1116).

Status and actions¶

Statuses are in_preparation (default), submitted, awarded, completed, rejected, and cancelled (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/models.py:54-62). The service maps each action to a target status and allowed source statuses (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:57-64).

In prose: a quotation is prepared and submitted, then awarded or rejected. An awarded quotation is completed or cancelled. A rejected or cancelled quotation can be reopened. revise works from submitted, rejected, or cancelled and starts a new in_preparation version (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:509-525).

Business rules¶

  • A quotation can be edited only in in_preparation or submitted (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:54, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:634-640).
  • Only in_preparation, cancelled, or rejected quotations can be deleted; delete sets deleted_at (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:224-238).
  • Submitting needs at least one line, at least one line that is not rejected, a currency, and a validity date (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:599-633).
  • The effective unit price walks the conditions in sequence order. A percent applies to the running subtotal or to the net price; per_unit adds a fixed amount per unit; absolute spreads a fixed amount over the quantity. Discounts subtract, surcharges and freight add, and the result is floored at zero (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:797-831).
  • The line total is quantity times effective price, rounded to four places; the header total is recomputed on every line or condition change (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:848-874).
  • Award stores the decision first, then creates a follow-on purchase order or contract when asked, and skips this if a follow-on already exists (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:321-339, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:389-449).
  • code comes from the yearly sequence purchasing_quotation; a revision gets a new code and version_no plus one (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:168, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:522).
  • Scales are stored per line and copied on revision; the effective price calculation does not read them (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:580-582, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:848-851).

Privacy, retention, and history¶

The module has no privacy_handlers.py. Quotations are soft-deleted (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:236). Pricing conditions and scales are child rows that are replaced together with their line; they are removed through the ORM so that each removal is recorded (see Notes below). Changes are captured by change history.

Analytics facts¶

analytics_facts.py contributes the fact set purchasing_quotation with the measures quotation_count, total_amount, median_amount, and average_lead_time_days, read with purchasing_quotation.read (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/analytics_facts.py:17-117). See analytics facts.

Discrepancies¶

Note

Deleting a pricing condition or a scale calls session.delete (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:918-940, src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:970-989). This is one of the sanctioned exceptions to the no-physical-delete rule: dependent pricing rows that are replaced together with their owner are removed through the ORM, and the change history records every ORM delete, so the removal stays traceable.

Warning: Discrepancy. The awarded event sends product_model_id and product_version_id as None when a line lacks them, and the receiving handler skips lines without a full breakdown (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:350-371, src/cluster_erp/modules/procurement/module.py:825-833). A failure while emitting the event is logged with its traceback and does not undo the saved award (src/cluster_erp/modules/purchasing_quotations/quotations/services.py:372-387). This page documents the code.
