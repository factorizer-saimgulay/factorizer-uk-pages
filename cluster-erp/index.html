Cluster ERP

Cluster ERP is our modular enterprise resource planning software, built for companies whose work is driven by projects and production. It covers the ground a production business needs, with the eBOM, MRP and MES at its centre, and because we build it ourselves it is shaped around how each business actually runs.

What modular means in practice

A project- and production-driven company adopts its ERP in its own order. Modular means the system is taken on in stages: start with the area that matters most, run it properly, then extend into the next area while everything already in place keeps running.

Modules share one data foundation, so every extension adds capability on the same records.

Module areas

  • eBOM: the engineering bill of materials everything else is built from
  • Project and works order management
  • MRP: material requirements and production planning
  • MES: shop floor execution and tracking
  • Procurement, inventory and materials
  • Quality, traceability and documentation
  • Costing, commercial and reporting

Who it suits

Engineering and manufacturing businesses running to order, where jobs are individually costed and the work calls for an ERP shaped around projects.
