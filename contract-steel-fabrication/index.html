Contract Steel Fabrication

Steel, stainless and aluminium, fabricated and welded to order.

Structural fabrication and welding by MIG, TIG and robotic cells, from a single assembly to large series. Work is produced in a production environment certified to EN 1090-2 EXC3, EN ISO 3834-2, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.

Execution class held by the production environment.
EN ISO 3834-2

Production environment

Made where the standards are held.

Our fabrication is produced in a production environment certified to the standards below. The certificates are held where the work is done and can be evidenced on request.

ISO 9001:2015
Quality management: how work is controlled, recorded and repeated consistently.
ISO 14001:2015
Environmental management: how environmental impact is identified and controlled.
ISO 45001:2018
Occupational health and safety: how risk to people at work is managed.
EN ISO 3834-2
Comprehensive quality requirements for fusion welding of metallic materials.
EN 1090-2 EXC3
Execution of steel structures at execution class 3, applied to demanding structural work.
