A compact truck that picks up a semi-trailer by its kingpin and moves it around the yard without a road tractor. The lifting fifth wheel raises the trailer off its legs; a strobe beacon and a horn warn the yard. Every figure above is a setting: change it in the configurator and the machine is redrawn, then request a quotation for what you set.

This setting

What is on the machine, and where it fits.

On the machine

  • Lifting fifth wheel on the deck
  • One amber strobe beacon
  • Horn

Where it fits

  • Trailer movements in a distribution yard
  • Dock to dock shunting at a port or a plant
  • Any yard where a road tractor is the bottleneck

Built to order, for any sector. What is fixed to the deck sets the job; the platform is the same. Read what it does on the transporters page.
