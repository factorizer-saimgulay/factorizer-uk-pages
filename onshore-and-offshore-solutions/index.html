Transport, installation and service tools

Frames that carry the component from the hall to the site.

Large components such as rotor blades, nacelles, hubs, generators and towers travel on purpose-built transport, installation and service tools. They are designed for safe, efficient movement inside the production facility and onward to the installation site, on land or at sea.

  • Available for both onshore and offshore transport
  • Steel or aluminium structures, to the component and the route
  • Hot and cold rolled profiles of certified quality
  • First-class transport dampers, load rings and connection parts
  • Liquid coating, hot-dip galvanising or metallising
  • Produced within tight tolerances

Production and storage tools

Stands, cradles and storage tools shaped to the part.

Between the stations of a line and in the yard, a component rests on tools made for it. Stands, cradles and storage frames, with adjustable supports and premium pads where they meet the surface.

  • Custom design for the component and the line
  • Structures fabricated to drawing
  • Height and width adjustable
  • Premium pads at every contact point
  • Hot-dip galvanised or liquid coated

Rotor blade turning device

Turns a rotor blade on the line, under the standard crane.

Rotating a large, delicate blade during production calls for exact control. The turning device does it in two coordinated units. A root unit carries the drive, for balanced and safe rotation. A tip unit supports the blade and keeps it guided throughout.

  • Moves and turns rotor blades along the production hall
  • Root and tip units with a combined capacity of 45 t
  • Operates under the standard overhead crane
  • Remote steering
  • Drum design for safer rotation
  • Automatic balance available, with sensor control and a counterweight system

Blade root curing heater

Controlled curing at the blade root.

Resin at the blade root cures under controlled temperature and air flow, for structural durability and consistent quality. The heater docks to the root, holds the heat with its insulation and silicone sealing, and folds for transport between lines and sites.

  • Adjustable air flow, 6300 m³/h in total
  • Adjustable temperature, 0 to 100 °C
  • Silicone sealing for maximum efficiency
  • Insulated to hold the heat where it is needed
  • Foldable for transport and quick setup on site
