Robotics, Cell Design and Simulation

Robot cells, proven in simulation, built to run.

Design and simulation

Every cell is proven in simulation first.

Layout, robot reach, cycle time and clearances are modelled before manufacture, and the end-of-arm tooling is designed around the part. Commissioning then confirms what the model already showed.

What a robot cell gives a production line.

Continuous operation around the clock, at a steady rate.

Flexibility

Reprogrammed for new tasks and added to existing lines smoothly.

Quality

Cost

Lower labour cost, less waste and rework, and a quick return.

Reliability

Special machine

Sealing plug handling machine

Seals valve holes automatically before the helium leak test, taking over a manual step and raising the reliability of the test. A double 3D camera system detects and measures each valve hole. Average capacity: 423 wheels per hour.

Design render

Special machine

Sprue drilling machine

Drills the sprue points of cast aluminium wheels, keeping each wheel true and crack-free. Integrated wheel detection, with an automatic tool changer as an option.
